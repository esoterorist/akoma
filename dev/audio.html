What you play to the rack comes back this much late. A recording is moved earlier by it, so the clip sits on the bar you played to. Measure needs the input to hear the Output: the mic near the speaker, or a cable from an output to this input. If recordings still land late, trim up; early, trim down.

Audio file onto the tape

WAV, MP3, M4A, FLAC or OGG, up to 10 minutes. It lands on the first bar of the song’s start section (the yellow line in the Arranger). Or drop a file here, or straight onto this track’s row in Song view.

Split takes a mix apart: drums on Audio 1, bass on Audio 2, vocals on Audio 3, the rest on Audio 4, on the same bar, fitted as set here. What is on those tapes there is replaced (⌘Z brings it back). The first split downloads the model, about 172 MB, and the browser keeps it. With WebGPU (Chrome, Edge) expect a minute or more for a four-minute track; without it, many times longer.
