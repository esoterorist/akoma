Slices split the sample evenly. Each step plays its own slice, so a one-bar break cut into 16 and synced to 1 bar plays back in time, and locking a step to another slice reshuffles it. Sync stretches the sample to the tempo by changing its speed, like a turntable. Double-tap a fader label to reset it.

Parameter locks

Move a fader here to lock that value to this step only. Locked faders turn yellow; double-tap the label to unlock. Conditions: 1:2 plays on the first of every two loops of the track, Fill plays only while Fill is held, Pre follows the last conditional step on this track, 1st plays only the first time through.

01—
Euclid

Each track has its own length and speed, so a 7-step perc against a 16-step hat drifts and comes back around. Euclid spreads the pulses as evenly as it can across the track's steps; Rotate moves the whole figure. Random and Humanize write into the steps, so Undo takes them back.

Repeat

Set A stores every track's sound, Set B stores another; the crossfader morphs between them. Repeat loops the last slice of time on the beat grid. Delay and Wash throw the mix into a tail that keeps ringing after you let go.

Nkonsonkonson NKN—08 sequencer and looper. Nkonsonkonson is the Adinkra symbol of chain links: things held together. Steps after Elektron parameter locks, trig conditions and per-track lengths; euclid after algorithmic sequencers; capture after the always-listening buffers of Ableton and Roland boxes; scenes after the Octatrack crossfader; punch-in effects after pocket samplers.
